Content & Research

What makes a stream worth watching.

We researched three possible streaming content types and created a survey to understand what students prefer. Our survey collected 46 responses from students across Grades 7–12.

01

Our Streaming Topic

Our chosen direction is a fun combined stream featuring food mukbang, horror storytelling, and a 6 Senses Cooking Challenge. The goal is to mix entertainment, storytelling, challenges, teamwork, and audience interaction.

02

Target Audience

Our target audience is mainly teenagers and students aged 13 and above, particularly students from Grades 7–12. They are interested in entertaining, relatable, interactive, and easy-to-follow content they can enjoy during their free time or after school.

03

Viewer Needs

Viewers need entertainment, relaxation, interaction, relatable experiences, interesting stories, and opportunities to learn practical skills. They also appreciate content that feels authentic and allows them to participate.

04

Characteristics of a Good Stream

A good stream should be entertaining, interactive, creative, relatable, organized, authentic, and consistent. Good audio and video quality, audience interaction, teamwork, and engaging activities can also help keep viewers interested.

05

Our Research Journey

We first researched common streaming content and selected Gaming, Food Mukbang + Horror Stories, and 6 Senses Cooking Challenge. We then created a survey to understand student preferences, viewing likelihood, viewing frequency, and audience interests. We analyzed 46 responses from Grades 7–12 and used the results to guide our content decision.

06

Why This Content?

The combined format allows us to bring together several types of entertainment in one stream. It can include food, storytelling, challenges, humor, teamwork, and audience participation while keeping the content interesting and varied.

What Students Chose

Survey results from 46 respondents across Grades 7–12.

Total respondents: 46

Gaming 28.3%
Combined: Mukbang + Horror + 6 Senses 28.3%
6 Senses Cooking Challenge 26.1%
Food Mukbang + Horror Stories 17.4%
Our Selected Direction

The Combined content received 13 out of 46 responses, or 28.3%, tying with Gaming for the highest selection. We chose the Combined concept because it brings together food, horror stories, and a 6 Senses challenge, giving viewers different activities and forms of entertainment in one stream.

Preferred Streaming Platforms

Recommended platforms based on the content format and target student audience.

01

YouTube

Suitable for full livestreams, gaming, cooking, storytelling, longer videos, and recorded episodes.

02

TikTok

Useful for short clips, funny moments, challenge highlights, horror-story clips, and promoting full streams.

03

Facebook

Supports livestreaming and audience interaction through comments, reactions, shares, and community engagement.
