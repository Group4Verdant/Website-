Content & Research

From ideas to content.

We researched different streaming formats and gathered responses from 51 students to understand what type of content viewers would enjoy. The research focused on entertainment, interaction, relatability, and the types of videos students watch during their free time.

01

Our Streaming Topic

Our content focuses on fun and interactive streams that combine food, horror stories, challenges, and entertainment.

02

Target Audience

Our target audience is teenagers and students, especially Grades 7–12, who enjoy entertaining, relatable, and interactive content during their free time.

03

Viewer Needs

Viewers look for entertainment, relaxation, fun challenges, interesting stories, audience participation, and content they can enjoy with friends.

04

Characteristics of a Good Stream

A good stream should be entertaining, interactive, relatable, creative, organized, authentic, and easy to follow, while also having clear audio and video.

05

Our Research Journey

We researched common streaming formats, selected several content ideas, surveyed 51 students, and analyzed their preferences and reasons for choosing each option.

06

Why This Content?

The combined food mukbang, horror stories, and 6 senses challenge format received 15 out of 51 responses, or 29.4%. It tied with Gaming as the most selected option.
